Roofing

Full Roof Replacement

For a roof at the end of its life, or one with damage in too many places to patch.

You probably need this if
Your roof is 20+ years old, you're patching leaks repeatedly, shingles are curling or losing their granules, or the roof is sagging.
What happens
The existing roof is removed so the wood decking beneath can be fully inspected and any damaged areas addressed. Ice and water shield is then installed at valleys and other vulnerable areas where needed, followed by roofing felt or underlayment. Your selected shingles or roofing material are installed next, with new flashing completed at chimneys, skylights, walls, and other transitions. We finish with a thorough cleanup of the jobsite.

Roof Leak Advice

Most roof leaks start at a detail—not in the middle of the roof.

A ceiling stain is often only where water finally appears. The source may be higher up at a chimney, skylight, wall joint, valley, or roof edge.

  • Cracked or missing flashing can let water behind the roofing system.
  • Skylight seals and surrounding shingles need to work as one waterproof detail.
  • Overflowing gutters can push water back toward fascia, roof edges, and walls.

Safety & Training

Professional work begins with a safe, prepared crew.

Every project is approached with the safety of our crew, your home, and your family in mind. Our team is trained to work carefully, protect the property, and maintain an orderly jobsite from start to finish.

01 — Safety standards

OSHA-compliant practices.

Our crew follows OSHA-compliant work practices, jobsite awareness, and safe procedures designed for residential roofing work.

02 — Fall protection

NRCA fall protection training.

We use NRCA fall protection course principles, appropriate fall-arrest systems, and controlled work areas to help protect the crew at elevation.

03 — Torch work

CERTA-trained application.

CERTA—Certified Roofing Torch Applicator Program—training supports safe, informed handling of torch-applied roofing work when that system is required.

04 — Access

Ladder and access protocols.

Ladders, roof access points, and equipment placement are reviewed before work begins, helping keep the jobsite controlled and secure.

05 — Equipment

Proper tools, properly used.

Every crew member is trained to use roofing products, power tools, and jobsite equipment safely and with care around your home.

06 — Daily standards

A safety culture, every day.

Safety is not a one-time checklist. It is part of how the crew communicates, works, cleans, and looks out for the property throughout the job.

Uniformed and respectful

Our uniformed crew represents Precision professionally and treats your home, driveway, landscaping, and neighbors with respect.

Cleaned daily

We keep the site organized each day, remove debris, and do not leave dangerous materials or loose tools around the property.

Protection beyond the roof

From setup through final cleanup, we work to leave your property safe, tidy, and ready to enjoy when the job is complete.
